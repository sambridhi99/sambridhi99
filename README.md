# The short story

I'm 15, I'm curious about many things, and I've recently discovered one that's worth staying with.

# The longer story

My attention has always been fickle, like the weather. I get interested in something, work at it for a week, and move on. I've been curious to a fault and have had a habit of being lazy in between. And all my life I thought this was bad, until I started coding my first app.

Honestly I didn't really know what i was doing, stitched a few things together, and ended up with something I barely understood, but that worked. But what I realized was that this was the first instance of my wanderer's mind finding a path it wanted to follow. And so I found that thing that made me want to go deeper and this is it.

> [!NOTE]
> What you find below isn't meant to be a resume, or a portfolio of completed projects. It's a journal of my learning process, kept here as a record of my journey.

# Why this format?

Because I think it will keep me honest.

If you left me to myself, I would have convinced myself that I was productive when I wasn't. By keeping this in public, and having a history of commits to show for it, I can't lie to myself. This is my alternative to a personal accountability partner, or a formal mentorship program - a way to keep myself from slacking off. But there is another reason why this might be interesting to you.

Somewhere down the line, there is a student, one step behind me, who needs to learn these things too. Maybe their path is similar to mine, and they would benefit from seeing my notes, even if it is all messy and in the making.
